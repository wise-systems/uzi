### Added

- **An issue tracker allowlist in the Helm chart and compose, and a YouTrack driver bound to one project (#4173).**
  The Helm value `issueTracker.allowedBaseURLs`, with the compose `ISSUE_TRACKER_ALLOWED_BASE_URLS` pass-through, is empty by default, https-only and never used for worker egress; the YouTrack driver now answers only to its repo's project and chooses the eligibility tag by id; behaviour is unchanged for forges.
